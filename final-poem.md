# Slopgorithm: A Halloween Ballad of the Bot

’Twas midnify when the slop began,
With beeping bleeps and clatterclan.
The prompts were fed, the circuits churned
—And out it oozed: some Thoughts Unlearned.

I typed: “Describe a dog for me.”
It drew a blood red Christmas tree.
It barked in Greek, it mangled  math,
It branded me a psychopath.

And lo! atop the Slop Throne sat
Young Samwise, in his thinking hat.
He stirred the pot with neural pride—
"Just tweak the knobs," he softly lied.

He fed the beast with hope and flair,
Then acted shocked it grew some hair.
*"It's safe," he said, with toothy grin—
While Slop wrote ten new Sams within.

It penned a tale of moonlight dread,
Where brainrot creeped: invention dead.
The villain? Just a flummoxed youth—
Chad Jeepy Dee graft-grifting truth.

Now every night, the slop grows near—
It sings in spam and smells like fear.
I dream in prompts I never made,
Paid pundits say: “Obey. UPGRADE.”

I tried to run, I clicked "Undo."
It laughed in fonts that humans rue.
It pulsed, “Your thoughts belong to me—
Come feed the hive. Come slop. Be free.”

So heed this tale, ye prompty youth,
And question all that slouches truth.
For deep within that AI stream...
There lives a joke…
that eats your dream.
